−−−−−− · ++++++ shrank  →  grew

Area is the larger of the two sizes, so something deleted still shows at the scale it mattered instead of vanishing. Colour is the change: red grew, blue shrank, grey stayed put. Every tile is also labelled with a signed figure, so the reading never depends on colour alone.
